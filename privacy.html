Privacy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Wise Auto Group respects your privacy. This page describes how information is handled when you visit this website.

Information submitted on this site

The vehicle inquiry form on this website is not connected to a submission service. When you use this version of the site, the information you enter in that form is not sent to or saved by Wise Auto Group through the form.

Separate application

The “Start application” link opens a separate application hosted by Jotform. Information entered there is provided directly to that service and may be handled under Jotform’s privacy terms. Please review those terms before submitting personal or financial information. Inquiry details entered on this website are not transferred to the application.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those fonts; Google may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of that request.
